# PayFlex Pay-in-3 — Product Overview

PayFlex Pay-in-3 lets an eligible customer split an eligible purchase into
**three equal monthly instalments at zero additional cost**. The customer pays
exactly the cart value — no interest, no convertible charge, no markup.

## How it works

1. Customer selects **PayFlex Pay-in-3** at checkout on a partner merchant.
2. The first instalment (one third of cart value) is charged immediately.
3. The remaining two instalments are auto-debited on the **same calendar date**
   in each of the next two months.

## What "zero-cost" means precisely

The customer repays exactly the purchase amount, split three ways. PayFlex earns
its margin from the merchant, not the customer. There is no interest component
and no processing fee on a Pay-in-3 transaction that is repaid on schedule.

Zero-cost applies **only** when instalments are paid on time. Late payment
charges are a separate matter — see `late-payment-policy`.

## Transaction limits

- Minimum cart value: **₹3,000**
- Maximum cart value per transaction: **₹60,000**
- A customer may hold up to **3 active Pay-in-3 plans** at once, subject to their
  approved limit.

## What this product is not

- It is not a personal loan and cannot be disbursed as cash.
- It is not a credit card and carries no revolving balance.
- It cannot be used at non-partner merchants.
