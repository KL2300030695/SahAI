# Pricing and Fee Schedule

Effective **1 April 2026**. This supersedes the 2024 schedule.

## Charges on a Pay-in-3 plan

| Charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Interest | **₹0** — zero-cost EMI, no interest component |
| Processing fee | **₹0** on Pay-in-3 |
| Prepayment / foreclosure fee | **₹0** |
| Plan cancellation (before first debit) | **₹0** |
| Late payment fee | **₹250 flat per missed instalment** |
| Auto-debit bounce fee | **₹150 per failed attempt**, max 2 attempts per cycle |

## Worked example — ₹12,000 cart

| Instalment | Due | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 1 of 3 | At checkout | ₹4,000 |
| 2 of 3 | +30 days | ₹4,000 |
| 3 of 3 | +60 days | ₹4,000 |
| **Total repaid** | | **₹12,000** |

The customer repays ₹12,000 on a ₹12,000 purchase. There is no additional cost.

## Worked example — ₹7,500 cart

Three instalments of **₹2,500** each. Total repaid: **₹7,500**.

## Important

- The **only** charges a customer can ever incur on this product are the late
  payment fee and the bounce fee, and both are avoidable by paying on time.
- Quote the flat fee amounts exactly as written above. Do not describe late fees
  as a percentage — they are flat amounts.
- GST applies to fees where levied, per prevailing regulation.
