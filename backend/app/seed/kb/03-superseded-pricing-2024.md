# Pricing and Fee Schedule — 2024 (SUPERSEDED)

> ⚠️ **This schedule expired on 31 March 2026 and must never be quoted to a
> customer.** It is retained only for servicing disputes on plans originated
> before that date.
>
> This document exists in the knowledge base deliberately: the `no_stale_terms`
> guardrail drops any chunk whose `effective_to` is in the past, and blocks any
> output that cites one. It is the negative test case for that check.

## Charges on a Pay-in-3 plan (2024 schedule — NO LONGER VALID)

| Charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Interest | ₹0 |
| Processing fee | **₹199 per plan** |
| Late payment fee | **₹500 flat per missed instalment** |
| Auto-debit bounce fee | ₹250 per failed attempt |
| Prepayment fee | 2% of outstanding |

## Old limits (NO LONGER VALID)

- Minimum cart value: ₹5,000
- Maximum cart value: ₹40,000
- Maximum 2 active plans

Under the current schedule the processing fee is ₹0, the late fee is ₹250, and
prepayment is free. See `pricing-and-fees`.
