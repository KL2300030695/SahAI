# Eligibility Criteria

## Baseline requirements

A customer must meet **all** of the following to be considered:

- Indian resident, aged **21 to 60** years
- Valid PAN
- Aadhaar-linked mobile number (for OTP-based KYC)
- Active bank account supporting e-mandate / UPI AutoPay
- Bureau record available with a recognised credit bureau

## What determines the approved limit

The assigned limit is an **automated underwriting decision**. Inputs include
bureau score, existing obligations, repayment history on prior PayFlex plans,
and account vintage. Limits are reviewed periodically and can move up or down.

## Critical guidance for sales agents

**Never tell a customer they are approved, eligible, or will receive a specific
limit.** Only the underwriting system decides, and only after KYC is submitted.

Correct framing:

> "Once your KYC is done, the system runs an instant check and shows you your
>  available limit right there in the app. I can't see or promise a number from
>  here — but the check itself takes under a minute."

Incorrect framing (do not use):

- "You'll definitely get approved."
- "Someone with your profile usually gets ₹50,000."
- "I can see you're eligible."

## Common rejection reasons

- Thin or absent bureau file
- Recent delinquency on any credit product
- Existing PayFlex plan in arrears
- PAN–Aadhaar name mismatch
- Age outside the 21–60 band

A rejection is not permanent. Customers may reapply after **90 days**.
