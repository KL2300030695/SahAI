# KYC and Onboarding — Step by Step

Typical completion time: **under 4 minutes** end to end.

## The five steps

1. **Mobile verification** — enter the Aadhaar-linked mobile number, receive an
   OTP, enter it. (~30 seconds)
2. **PAN entry** — type the PAN. Name is auto-fetched and shown for confirmation.
   (~30 seconds)
3. **Aadhaar e-KYC** — consent to Aadhaar-based verification, receive a second
   OTP on the same number, enter it. (~45 seconds)
4. **Bank mandate setup** — select the bank, authorise a UPI AutoPay or
   e-NACH mandate for auto-debit of instalments. (~90 seconds)
5. **Instant limit decision** — the underwriting check runs and the available
   limit is displayed in-app. (~30 seconds)

## Where customers actually drop off

Measured drop-off by step:

| Step | Drop-off rate | Usual cause |
|---|---|---|
| 3 — Aadhaar e-KYC | **31%** | Second OTP feels like friction; privacy hesitation |
| 4 — Bank mandate | **24%** | Reluctance to authorise auto-debit |
| 2 — PAN entry | 9% | PAN not to hand |

Steps 3 and 4 account for over half of all abandonment. These are the two moments
worth pre-empting on a call.

## Pre-empting step 3

> "There's a second OTP for the Aadhaar step — that's the standard RBI-mandated
>  check, same as any bank account opening. It's the one place people pause, so
>  I'll stay on the line while you do it."

## Pre-empting step 4

> "The mandate is what lets instalments 2 and 3 debit automatically so you never
>  have to remember a date. You can cancel it from the app any time, and it only
>  ever debits the exact instalment amount — nothing else."

## Resuming an abandoned application

Progress is saved for **7 days**. The customer resumes from the exact step they
left, at the same link — no restart.
