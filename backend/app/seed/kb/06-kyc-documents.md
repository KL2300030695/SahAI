# Accepted KYC Documents

## Mandatory

- **PAN** — the number itself; no upload or photograph required.
- **Aadhaar** — verified via OTP e-KYC. The customer never uploads an Aadhaar
  image and never shares the full number with a PayFlex agent.

## Not required

Customers frequently expect these and are relieved to hear they are not needed:

- Salary slips or Form 16
- Bank statements
- Employer letter
- Physical signature
- Address proof (fetched from Aadhaar)
- Photograph or selfie video (for cart values under ₹60,000)

## Name mismatch

If the PAN name and Aadhaar name differ (common after marriage, or with initials
spelled out), the customer resolves it by updating either record with the issuing
authority. PayFlex cannot override the mismatch. Expect a 5–7 working day delay
once the record is corrected.

## Agent instruction — never collect these on a call

Sales agents must **never** ask a customer to read out over the phone:

- Full Aadhaar number
- OTP (of any kind, for any reason)
- Card number, CVV, or PIN
- Net-banking password

PayFlex staff will never ask for an OTP. If a customer offers one, stop them and
say so explicitly. Record the interaction.
