# Late Payment Policy

## Charges

- **₹250 flat** per missed instalment. This is a flat amount, not a percentage,
  and it does not compound.
- **₹150** per failed auto-debit attempt (maximum 2 attempts per cycle).
- **No interest accrues on a late instalment.** The product carries no interest
  component at any point, including in delinquency.

There is a **3-day grace period** after the due date before the late fee is
applied.

## Escalation timeline

| Days past due | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Grace period. Reminders only, no fee. |
| 4 | ₹250 late fee applied. SMS + email. |
| 7 | Further Pay-in-3 purchases blocked until cleared. |
| 30 | Reported to credit bureaus as overdue. |
| 60 | Account referred to recovery. |

## Bureau reporting — answer this one accurately

On-time repayment is reported and **can help** build credit history. A missed
instalment is reported at **30 days past due** and can hurt the customer's score.

Do not soften this. A customer who is surprised later is a complaint and a
churn risk. The honest framing:

> "If you pay on time — and it's auto-debited, so most people do — it's reported
>  positively and actually helps your credit history. If an instalment goes 30
>  days unpaid, that does get reported and it can affect your score. That's the
>  straight answer."

## Hardship

Customers in genuine difficulty should be transferred to the **collections
support team**, who can restructure. A sales agent must not offer, imply, or
negotiate a waiver, deferral, or restructure. Transfer, don't improvise.
