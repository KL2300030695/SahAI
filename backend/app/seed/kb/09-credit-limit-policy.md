# Credit Limit Assignment and Review

## Range

Assigned limits fall between **₹5,000 and ₹1,50,000**. The specific limit is set
by the automated underwriting model at the end of KYC and is visible to the
customer in-app immediately.

## What an agent may and may not say

**May not:** predict, estimate, promise, or hint at a specific limit. Not "you'll
probably get around ₹40,000", not "customers like you usually see ₹50,000", not
"I think you'll be fine".

**May:** describe the range as published above, and describe when the customer
will see their own number.

The distinction matters legally, not just commercially. A predicted limit is
read as an offer.

## Limit reviews

- Automatic review every **90 days**.
- On-time repayment across a completed plan is the strongest positive input.
- Limits can decrease if bureau data deteriorates or an instalment goes unpaid.
- Customers may request a manual review from the app once per **180 days**.

## Utilisation

The limit is shared across all active plans. A customer with a ₹30,000 limit and
₹18,000 outstanding has ₹12,000 available. Repayment frees the limit immediately
on settlement of each instalment.
