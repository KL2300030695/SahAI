# Merchant Coverage and Eligible Purchases

## Where Pay-in-3 works

Over **4,200 partner merchants**, online and in-store, across:

- Electronics and mobile
- Fashion and footwear
- Home and furniture
- Travel bookings
- Health, optical, and dental
- Education and upskilling platforms

The current merchant list is in the app under **Shop → Partner stores**, and is
the only authoritative source. Do not recite merchant names from memory on a
call — coverage changes weekly.

## Excluded categories

Pay-in-3 cannot be used for:

- Gold, bullion, and investment products
- Cryptocurrency and securities
- Gambling and lottery
- Cash withdrawal or wallet top-up
- Utility bills, rent, insurance premiums
- Peer-to-peer transfers

## In-store

In-store purchases work by scanning a PayFlex QR at the till. The instalment
plan is confirmed in-app before payment completes.

## If the customer's merchant is not covered

Be straight about it and offer the alert:

> "That one isn't a partner yet. You can tap 'Request a store' in the app — we
>  do use that list when we prioritise, and you'll get a notification if it goes
>  live."
