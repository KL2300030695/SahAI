# Cancellations, Returns and Refunds

## Cancelling a plan before the first debit

Free, instant, from the app. The limit is released immediately.

## Returning a purchase mid-plan

When a merchant confirms a return, PayFlex is notified automatically.

- **Full return** — remaining instalments are cancelled and any amount already
  paid is refunded to the original payment method. The plan closes.
- **Partial return** — remaining instalments are recalculated downward. The
  revised schedule appears in-app within 48 hours of merchant confirmation.

## Refund timelines

| Method | Timeline |
|---|---|
| UPI | 1–3 working days |
| Debit / credit card | 5–7 working days |
| Net banking | 3–5 working days |

Timelines start from **merchant confirmation of the return**, not from the date
the customer shipped the item. This distinction is the single most common source
of refund complaints — state it plainly up front.

## Fees on a returned purchase

Any late fee already levied is **not** reversed by a return. The fee was incurred
for a missed instalment, which is a separate event from the return.

## Merchant disputes

If the merchant disputes the return, the instalment schedule continues while the
dispute is open. Transfer these to the **disputes team** — a sales agent must
not adjudicate, promise an outcome, or pause a schedule.
