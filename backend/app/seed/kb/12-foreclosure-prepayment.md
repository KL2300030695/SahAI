# Prepayment and Foreclosure

## Prepaying a single instalment

Any upcoming instalment can be paid early from the app at **zero charge**. The
auto-debit for that instalment is then skipped.

## Closing the plan entirely

Paying the full outstanding closes the plan immediately.

- **Foreclosure fee: ₹0.** There is no penalty for early closure.
- No interest is saved by prepaying, because no interest was ever charged. The
  customer pays exactly the same total either way.
- The limit is released immediately on closure.

## Answering "should I just pay it off early?"

Be honest — there is no financial advantage, only a preference:

> "You can, and there's no charge for it — but you won't save anything either,
>  because there's no interest to save. Same total either way. Some people just
>  prefer having it closed; others prefer keeping the cash and letting it
>  auto-debit. Genuinely your call."

Do not manufacture a benefit that does not exist. A customer who later works out
that early closure saved them nothing will not trust the next thing you say.

## Effect on credit profile

A plan closed early is reported as **closed, paid in full**, which is a positive
record. It does not carry the negative connotation that early closure of an
interest-bearing loan sometimes does.
