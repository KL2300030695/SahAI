# FAQ — General

**Q: Do I need a credit card?**
No. Pay-in-3 works from a bank account via UPI AutoPay or e-NACH. A credit card
is neither required nor used.

**Q: How long does signup take?**
Under 4 minutes for most customers. See `kyc-process` for the step breakdown.

**Q: Can I use it on any website?**
Only at partner merchants — currently over 4,200. The live list is in the app
under Shop → Partner stores.

**Q: Is there really no interest?**
Correct. The customer repays exactly the cart value in three equal parts.
PayFlex earns from the merchant. The only possible charges are the ₹250 late fee
and the ₹150 bounce fee, both avoidable.

**Q: What if I want to pay it off early?**
Free, any time, from the app. No foreclosure fee. See `foreclosure-prepayment`.

**Q: What if I return the item?**
Remaining instalments are cancelled or recalculated once the merchant confirms
the return. See `cancellation-refunds`.

**Q: Can I change my due date?**
Once per plan, by up to 7 days, from the app, at least 24 hours before the debit.

**Q: How many plans can I run at once?**
Up to 3 active plans, within your approved limit.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
PayFlex is a regulated entity and handles personal and financial data under the
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. See `data-privacy-consent`.

**Q: What if I lose my phone?**
Call support to freeze the account. Instalment schedules are unaffected and
continue to auto-debit from the linked bank account.
