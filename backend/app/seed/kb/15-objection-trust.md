# Objection Handling — Trust, Credit Score and Privacy

## "Will this hurt my credit score?"

Answer both directions honestly. A one-sided answer here is the fastest way to
lose a customer permanently.

> "Two parts to that. Paying on time is reported positively and can help build
>  your history — and since it's auto-debited, most people do. If an instalment
>  goes 30 days unpaid, that's reported too and it can hurt your score. So it
>  helps if you pay on time and hurts if you don't, same as any credit product."

Do not say "it won't affect your credit score." That is false.

## "Is there a hard enquiry?"

The eligibility check at KYC is a **soft enquiry** and does not affect the score.
A hard enquiry is recorded only when a limit is actually assigned and the
customer accepts it.

## "I don't want to share my Aadhaar"

> "Understood. You never upload it or read it out — it's an OTP check, the same
>  one you'd do opening a bank account. We're a regulated entity and it's the
>  RBI-mandated method. It's also the reason you don't need salary slips or
>  bank statements."

## "How do I know you're not a scam?"

> "Reasonable thing to check. Don't take my word for it — go to the app store,
>  look at the listing and the reviews, and start from there rather than from
>  anything I send you. And note: we will never ask you for an OTP. If anyone
>  calls claiming to be us and asks for one, that's a fraud, and you should
>  report it."

Offering the customer a way to verify *independently of you* is more persuasive
than any reassurance.

## "I need to think about it"

Accept it. Do not press.

> "Of course. Nothing expires — the offer's in the app whenever you're ready.
>  Would it help if I sent the fee breakdown in writing so you've got it to
>  hand?"

Capture the real reason in the CRM. "Wants to think" is not a reason; "worried
about credit score impact" is, and it is what makes the follow-up useful.

## Hard rule

If a customer states they are not interested, stop selling. Log the reason,
confirm no further calls if they ask, and close warmly.
